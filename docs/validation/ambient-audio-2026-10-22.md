# CC0 house and yard ambience

Implemented the three approved recordings in the saved `CSI_Environment` scene:

- Yard: **Residential Street Ambience Quiet Birds.wav**, mzui, [Freesound 135074](https://freesound.org/people/mzui/sounds/135074/).
- House: **Room tone Very quiet Small apartment room loopable edlarez vsnr.wav**, visionear, [Freesound 565535](https://freesound.org/people/visionear/sounds/565535/).
- Quiet outdoor layer: **Wind in the Trees**, Joseph SARDIN, [BigSoundBank 0904](https://bigsoundbank.com/forest-wind-in-the-trees-s0904.html).

Each source explicitly lists CC0. Original acquired MP3s, source/license evidence, creators, processing details and original/derived SHA-256 hashes are in `docs/licenses/ambient-audio-2026-10-22/manifest.json`. Freesound's public HQ previews were used because original WAV downloads require login. BigSoundBank's public MP3 download was used for the tree recording; FFmpeg decoded its MP3 after soundfile rejected the header. The derived PCM16 WAVs are not original lossless recordings.

Stereo is preserved. Overlap crossfades produce loops of approximately 73.33 seconds for the yard, 20.31 seconds for the room and 58 seconds for the tree layer. The tree loop uses a quieter 60-second excerpt. Gain is bounded toward RMS .04, with at most 16x gain and a .8 peak cap. Media checks found no clipping or invalid samples; loop-boundary sample differences remain below .02 before the quiet runtime mix.

## Saved editable setup

The **Scene ambience - CC0 house and yard** object owns a `SceneAmbience` component, three AudioSources and two exterior low-pass filters. Everything is editable in the Inspector. Reauthor through **Tools > ViCSI > Scene Setup > Install CC0 Scene Ambience**.

- The existing house footprint, including its front wing and upstairs, determines the indoor mix. No new trigger volumes, colliders, UI, access rules or waypoints were added.
- Outdoors, residential ambience plays at .22 and tree rustle at .07. Inside, room tone blends toward .18; exterior beds retain 10% of their outdoor level and are muffled toward 1200 Hz.
- Transitions use a 1.8-second smoothing time constant. Dialogue, timed guidance and modal UI smoothly lower all beds to 55%, recovering when they close. Mixing uses unscaled delta time.
- The three sources use stereo streaming Vorbis at quality .65, priority 200, no Doppler and no reverb-zone send. They stop when disabled/unloaded and restart without allocating extra voices.
- The controller belongs to this scenario, with no persistent bootstrap into the lobby. Existing interaction sounds and excluded zipper/door/cabinet wiring remain unchanged. No scoring or timeline events are generated by the ambience.
- Unrelated editor-generated UI RectTransform serialization changes were restored from the pre-task on-disk scene snapshot.
