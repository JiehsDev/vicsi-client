# Cordon placement reference review — 2026-10-20

Review only. No scene layout or gameplay changes.

The entry posts are at (16.2, 0.5, 2.7) and (17.6, 0.5, 2.7). The front doorway frame is centered near (16.72, 2.25, 6.33). The tape is therefore approximately 3.6 m ahead of the doorway, before the front steps.

The visible front tape runs from X=14.5 to X=20.8 at Z=2.7, including the controlled entry. Native front and overhead views show that its ends stop in the lawn rather than connecting to the property fences. The game separately enforces a protected footprint; this does not close the visible gaps. Every side/rear route and fence collider was not walk-tested in this review.

References:

- [NIJ crime-scene training reference](https://www.ojp.gov/pdffiles1/nij/200160.pdf), printed page 15, section A.5: protect the occurrence area and potential entry/exit paths, establish sufficiently large boundaries, use physical barriers or existing walls/gated areas, and record entry/exit.
- [NIJ: Protecting the Scene](https://nij.ojp.gov/nij-hosted-online-training-courses/firearms-examiner-training/module-06/crime-scene): limit access, assign an officer to scene control, secure the perimeter and keep an entry log. This online training page is archived.
- [PNP CSI SOP in the Supreme Court E-Library](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/11/56392): the official search excerpt describes the First Responder securing the area by cordoning it to prevent unauthorized entry. Full-page retrieval timed out during this review.

Interpretation: the references do not prescribe a universal distance or require tape on the house door. A yard-level controlled entry is reasonable for this scene. However, the present visible tape does not complete that boundary. I recommend connecting it to the existing side fences or otherwise closing the bypasses with visible barriers, preserving the entry/logging station and clean equipment area. This is a design inference from the references, not a requirement to use these exact coordinates.
