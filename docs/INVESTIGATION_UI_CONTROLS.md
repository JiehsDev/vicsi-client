# Investigation UI controls

Open **Settings** from the utility wheel to adjust **Player Height** and **Walking Speed**. Both use minus/plus buttons and a separate reset button, and save on this headset across scenario restarts and lobby visits. Walking speed changes in 10% steps from 50% to 200%; 100% restores the scene's normal pace. The camera still slows movement while aiming and restores the chosen speed when lowered. Use the existing scroll controls or thumbstick navigation to reach all settings.

The procedure panel shows one current action for the selected, already-photographed item. Other steps remain pending; a checkmark means the corresponding recorded action is finished. Blood recovery, preparation, sealing and storage are separate. A sealed bag still needs **B** storage before its checklist is complete.

For a scaled final photo, raise the camera with left **X**, aim at the prepared item and press right **A** to place the ruler. Frame the item, marker and whole ruler until the border turns green, then use the trigger. The camera prompt explains any remaining framing issue. **A** removes the ruler; **X** lowers the camera. Original photos use no ruler. The header names the subject and photo stage instead of showing video-recording indicators.

Open **Photo Album** from the utility menu after lowering the camera. The gallery labels each image and shows the selected photo's full subject, stage and UTC capture time. Scene-route photographs use their view names. Empty albums explain how to begin.

| Control | Gallery | Inspecting a photo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Right stick left/right | Previous/next image | Previous/next image; resets zoom |
| Right stick up/down | Previous/next row | Zoom out/in by moving down/up, from 1× to 4× |
| Left stick | — | Pan a zoomed image |
| A | Inspect selected image | Return to gallery |
| B | Close album | Close album |

Return the right stick to its centre between navigation or zoom steps. The gallery changes page when necessary to keep the selection visible. Photos retain their original proportions; black margins are expected when the viewing area has a different shape.

At the outside drying cabinet, **A** starts drying and a later **A** explicitly retrieves the paper package. Drying pauses while reading menus or dialogue or when the player leaves the station. Close menus and stay near the cabinet to resume; use the cabinet's ring for live progress. Seal the prepared package with the trigger, then press **B** to store it. Follow the existing supply prompts for fresh swabs and glove changes.

For Body A and Evidence 1–3, the Recorder offers six statements. Navigate with the thumbstick, press **B** to select or deselect, and press **A** to confirm exactly three. Confirmation locks the entry. Findings become available after recording completion independently of the chosen statements. The investigation shows no correctness or score feedback; the completed session dashboard shows a separate Recorder Documentation score out of 24 alongside the unchanged Deduction Score.

For development checks, **Scene Test Start > Evidence photography** has direct ruler-photo starts. **All evidence measured** leaves sampling and recording for the tester. The existing settings restart action reloads the selected preset.
