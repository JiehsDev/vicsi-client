Checked 22 October 2026 against the individual publisher pages. This records the original research shortlist, based on uploader descriptions and the existing house/yard setting. The user subsequently authorized implementation of the three primary candidates; downloads, saved scene setup, processing and validation are recorded in [the implementation report](../validation/ambient-audio-2026-10-22.md). Recommended initial mix: quiet residential ambience outdoors and quiet room tone indoors. Treat tree rustle as an optional low layer, not an additional loud bed. Keep dialogue, photography, measurement and handling sounds easy to hear. If implemented later, soften the exterior bed inside the house and crossfade at entry rather than abruptly switching tracks. Seamless looping, clipping checks and headset loudness balancing remain to be done.

Freesound lists login as required for the original WAV downloads. BigSoundBank explicitly permits downloading without an account; OpenGameArt links the WAV files directly. Do not treat a preview as an original lossless WAV.

## Licensing

Each shortlisted item explicitly lists CC0. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits copying, modification and commercial distribution without requesting permission or requiring creator attribution. BigSoundBank's individual [Wind in the Trees page](https://bigsoundbank.com/forest-wind-in-the-trees-s0904.html) also explicitly confirms that credit is optional.

For any eventual import, retain the item page, CC0 deed, acquisition date, creator/source URL, original-file SHA-256 and an edit log, following the existing interaction-SFX provenance convention. These are internal records, not required in-game credits. CC0 does not provide ownership warranties or clear third-party privacy/publicity rights; check recordings for recognizable speech or embedded music while auditioning.

Other researched recordings were omitted from the main selection because they introduce rain, conspicuous machinery, river sounds or synthesized musical layers that are not established by the current scene. No gameplay, scene, sound-bank or production-data changes were made for this research.
